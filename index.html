SharkyClips

Hunt your best moments.

A free, open-source app for streamers. Point it at a stream recording and it finds the best moments, writes titles and a description, and renders vertical clips ready for YouTube Shorts, TikTok or Reels. Everything runs on your own computer.

Download Read the guide

What it does

Finds the moments

Volume spikes, laughter, reaction words, chat bursts and in-game events become one ranked list of clip candidates.

Writes the text

A local AI model scores each clip and suggests titles, hashtags and a short description in 7 languages.

Renders 9:16 clips

Facecam on top, gameplay below, word-timed subtitles, music and your brand kit, on your own GPU.

Stays local

No account, no cloud, no telemetry. Your videos never leave your computer unless you upload them.

How SharkyClips uses YouTube

Uploading to YouTube is optional. If you connect your channel in Settings → YouTube, SharkyClips uses the official YouTube API Services to do exactly these things, and only when you ask for them:

ActionPermission (scope)When
Upload a clip you set up (title, description, tags, privacy, release time, playlist, thumbnail); change its details or release time, publish it early or delete it when you cancel it before it is public; show which channel is connected; read the views, likes and privacy status of videos uploaded with SharkyClipsyoutubeWhen you press Publish now or schedule a clip (uploaded in advance while your PC is on), edit or cancel it in the Library, open Settings, and about every 6 hours while the app runs
Read the average view percentage of those same videos, to learn which opening (hook) style keeps your viewers watchingyt-analytics.readonlyOnly for clips uploaded as part of a hook test, once they have been public for 48 hours

This data is stored only on your computer and is used only to show you your results and to improve the clip scores for your channel. SharkyClips has no server: the developers never receive, see or share your YouTube data, and nothing is used for ads. SharkyClips never reads other people's channels, your comments, your subscribers or your watch history. It only changes or deletes videos that were uploaded with SharkyClips, and only when you ask (for example when you edit or cancel a scheduled clip).

You can disconnect at any time in Settings → YouTube (this revokes the access token at Google), or remove the access in your Google account permissions. Read the full privacy policy.
