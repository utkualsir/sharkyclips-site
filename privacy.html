Privacy policy

Effective 3 November 2026

Short version: SharkyClips runs on your computer. It has no server, no account and no telemetry. Your videos, transcripts, clips and YouTube data stay on your computer. The people who make SharkyClips never receive any of it.

1. Who this applies to

This policy covers the SharkyClips desktop app (the "app") and this website. SharkyClips is a free, open-source project published on GitHub (the "developers", "we"). You install the app on your own computer and it runs there.

2. Data the app works with

Everything below is created and stored on your computer only, in the SharkyClips folder (workspace) and in your settings folder (~/.sharkyclips):

  • Videos you import and what the app makes from them: audio, transcripts, highlight lists, rendered clips, exports.
  • Chat replays of streams you import, and the live chat of your own stream if you turn on live chat reading. Messages and author names are kept on your computer to find moments where chat got excited, for "!clip" markers and to show chat bubbles in clips (links, swear words, phone numbers, e-mail addresses and @mentions are never shown). The live chat log is deleted after 14 days.
  • Live markers you set during a stream (a hotkey, "!clip" in chat): the time and who set it.
  • Your settings and preferences, such as language, title feedback (thumbs up/down), glossary, music library and brand kit.
  • View counts you enter yourself to calibrate the viral score.

Speech recognition and the AI model run locally (Whisper and Ollama). The app does not send your videos, transcripts or prompts to any AI service.

3. When the app goes online

Only for things you start or allow:

  • Downloading a video or its chat replay from a link you paste (Twitch, YouTube, Kick and other sites, through yt-dlp). These sites receive a normal request from your computer.
  • Downloading the AI models once (from Hugging Face and Ollama).
  • Checking for and installing updates from GitHub, when you click "Check for updates".
  • The optional YouTube connection described below.
  • Reading your Twitch chat during a stream, if you enter your channel name: a read-only, anonymous connection to Twitch's chat server. The app never posts anything.
  • Your own Telegram bot, if you connect one (phone approval): the app sends the cover picture, title and score of ready clips (and the clip itself when you tap Watch) to Telegram's Bot API and receives your button presses. Telegram processes this under its own privacy policy. The bot token is stored encrypted on your computer.
  • OBS on your own computer (its local WebSocket server), if it is turned on, to read the recording time of a marker.

The app contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code.

4. YouTube data

SharkyClips uses the YouTube API Services. Connecting a YouTube channel is optional. By connecting, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google processes your data under the Google Privacy Policy.

The app does not read the contents of your other videos, your comments, subscribers or watch history, or other people's channels (live chat is read only for your own live stream, only when you turn it on). It only changes or deletes videos that were uploaded with SharkyClips, and only when you ask (editing or cancelling a scheduled clip).

Where it is stored

On your computer only: the access and refresh token in ~/.sharkyclips/youtube/token.enc (encrypted with Windows DPAPI or a key in the macOS Keychain), your publishing plan in ~/.sharkyclips/publish/plan.json, upload records and statistics in each project's uploads.json, and the derived learning data in ~/.sharkyclips/calibration.json and ~/.sharkyclips/hook_tests.json. The app talks directly to Google's servers (googleapis.com); there is no SharkyClips server in between.

Sharing

We do not receive, sell, rent or share YouTube data or any other data. It is not used for advertising and not transferred to anyone. SharkyClips' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Disconnect in Settings → YouTube revokes the token at Google and deletes it from your computer.
  • You can also revoke access at any time on your Google account permissions page.
  • Calibration data can be cleared on the Stats page. Deleting a project's folder from the workspace deletes its upload records. The uninstaller offers to delete your settings folder, including all YouTube data. You can also delete the files listed above yourself at any time.

5. LAN mode

If you start the app in LAN mode, devices on your local network can open its interface. Set an access PIN or password in Settings so only you can. The PIN is stored only as a salted hash on your computer.

6. This website

This site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and has no analytics. GitHub may log your IP address when you visit (see the GitHub Privacy Statement), and the fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

7. Children

SharkyClips is not directed at children under 13. Uploading to YouTube requires a YouTube account, which has its own age requirements.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date, and the change is listed in the app's changelog.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub. For anything private, use the private reporting form.
